At [Couchbase](https://www.couchbase.com/), I build SDKs and connectors for its distributed database.

My recent research examines how compression affects multi-vector retrieval. In [_Most MaxSim Winners Flip, Retrieval Survives_](/publications/#lakhotia2026maxsim) (<span class="research-venue">ReNeuIR @ SIGIR 2026</span>), we show that sign coding changes most token-level MaxSim winners while largely preserving retrieval effectiveness.

Earlier, I worked on efficient multitask learning and conditional computation. [_UnoLoRA_](/publications/#kamalesh2024unolora) (<span class="research-venue">FITML @ NeurIPS 2024</span>) shares one low-rank adapter across tasks. Our [task-sampling study](/publications/#lakhotia2025sampling) examines how sampling strategies change a transformer's internal representations. I also built [_Baraat_](https://github.com/anirudhlakhotia/Baraat), a multilingual mixture-of-experts system for code-switched Indic inputs.

I studied Computer Science at [PES University](https://pes.edu), where I was advised by [Dr. Gowri Srinivasa](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=xmJ1KmsAAAAJ). Before university, I interned at Cisco, developing an event platform with a personalized recommendation system. I later built geospatial data infrastructure for Nokia's cellular network data.
